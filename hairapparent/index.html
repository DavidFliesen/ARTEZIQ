Hair Apparent

AI Hairstyle Preview

See your next hairstyle before you sit down in the salon chair. Hair Apparent is a browser-based AI makeover experience that lets you preview new haircut ideas on your own photograph and create a salon-ready stylist brief. Your original photos remain on your device, and you can bring your own OpenAI API key for photorealistic image generation.

  • AI hairstyle previews
  • Use your own photograph
  • Salon-ready stylist brief
  • Photos remain on your device
  • Bring your own OpenAI API key
  • Currently being developed for iOS, Android and the Web.
