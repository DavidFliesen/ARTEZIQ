Hand Over Foot

Classic Card Game

Rediscover the classic rummy-style fun of Hand and Foot Canasta in a polished digital card game. Play solo against a built-in AI opponent at three difficulty levels—Easy, Club, and Shark—on a rich green-felt card table designed for clear, comfortable play. It is best experienced on tablets and computers while remaining fully playable on smartphones.

  • Easy, Club, and Shark AI difficulty levels
  • Classic rummy-style gameplay
  • Polished green-felt table interface
  • Optimized for tablets and computers
  • Playable on smartphones
  • Currently being developed for iOS, Android and the Web.
