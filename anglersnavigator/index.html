Angler's Navigator

Tackle & Bait Edition

Enter where and when you're fishing and let Angler's Navigator do the research for you. Using Open-Meteo weather forecasts, NOAA tide predictions, millions of iNaturalist observations, AI, and semantic matching, Angler's Navigator recommends likely species, matched lures, and practical fishing techniques tailored to current conditions.

  • Open-Meteo weather forecasts
  • NOAA tide predictions
  • iNaturalist observations
  • Semantic lure matching
  • Fishing techniques
  • Being developed for iOS and Android
