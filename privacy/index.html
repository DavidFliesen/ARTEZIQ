Region-aware analytics: For visitors detected in the EEA, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, ARTEZIQ does not load Google Analytics until the visitor chooses “Allow analytics.” For visitors outside those regions, Google Analytics may load automatically unless the visitor has previously declined analytics. Declining analytics does not prevent use of the site, the Explorer Program, or surveys.

1. Who is responsible for your data

ARTEZIQ is the controller of personal information collected directly through this website and the ARTEZIQ Explorer Program. ARTEZIQ is based in the United States. Privacy questions and requests may be sent to arteziq.apps@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers arteziq.com, ARTEZIQ application-information pages, the Explorer Program application/NDA workflow, ARTEZIQ surveys hosted on this site, and the optional Google Analytics measurement described below. Individual ARTEZIQ apps may have additional privacy notices if their features collect different information.

3. Information we collect

Website analytics and regional consent

When analytics is active—automatically in countries where consent is not required, or following an affirmative choice in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland—Google Analytics 4 (GA4) may process information such as page URLs, referring pages, campaign parameters (for example UTM source/medium/campaign), browser/device information, approximate location derived by Google, and non-identifying interaction events such as an app link click or a successful Explorer submission. ARTEZIQ does not intentionally send names, email addresses, phone numbers, signatures, NDA text, or free-form Explorer responses to GA4.

Explorer Program applications and NDAs

The Explorer form currently collects:

  • full name, email address, phone number, city/state, and preferred contact method;
  • primary testing device and a description of relevant experience/intended use;
  • the app being tested;
  • NDA name, NDA email, acceptance date, handwritten signature, agreement ID, and signing timestamp;
  • browser user-agent information and limited form-timing/rate-limit data used to prevent abuse.

Explorer applicants must confirm that they are at least 18 years old. ARTEZIQ does not ask applicants to provide special-category personal data and applicants should not include sensitive personal information in free-form responses unless it is genuinely necessary.

Surveys

ARTEZIQ surveys may collect responses, comments, and—when requested or voluntarily provided—name and email address. Survey pages may also send limited browser and anti-abuse timing information to the survey backend. Survey responses are not sent to GA4.

5. Where information is processed and stored

The public website is served through GitHub Pages and uses a Porkbun-registered domain. Explorer and survey submissions are transmitted to Google Apps Script endpoints operated under ARTEZIQ’s Google account.

For Explorer submissions, the current backend generates/transmits the submitted application and signed NDA PDF through Google’s email services. A signed NDA copy is emailed to the applicant, and an application summary plus the signed NDA is emailed to ARTEZIQ’s administrative mailbox. The current backend code does not intentionally write Explorer form responses or signed PDFs to a separate database or Google Drive folder. Copies are therefore retained primarily in the relevant email accounts unless ARTEZIQ later implements an additional storage system.

The anti-abuse rate-limit cache is temporary. ARTEZIQ’s Explorer backend uses a non-reversible hash for the rate-limit key and keeps that temporary cache entry for approximately 15 minutes.

6. Who receives information

Personal information may be received by:

  • authorized ARTEZIQ personnel who need it to administer the Explorer Program or surveys;
  • Google, as the service provider supporting Apps Script, email delivery, and—only after consent—Google Analytics;
  • GitHub, as the website-hosting provider, for normal hosting/security logs and technical delivery;
  • the applicant, who receives their own signed NDA copy;
  • government authorities, courts, advisers, or other recipients where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

ARTEZIQ does not sell Explorer application data or signed NDAs.

7. International transfers

ARTEZIQ is based in the United States, so information submitted by a person in the EEA, United Kingdom, or Switzerland may be processed in the United States and other locations used by ARTEZIQ’s service providers. Where data-protection law requires a transfer safeguard, ARTEZIQ relies on applicable transfer mechanisms made available by its service providers, such as adequacy frameworks and/or standard contractual clauses where available.

8. How long we retain information

  • Explorer applications and ARTEZIQ’s copy of signed NDAs: normally for the duration of the Explorer relationship plus 3 years, then deleted unless a longer period is reasonably necessary for a legal claim, legal obligation, or active dispute.
  • Explorer anti-abuse rate-limit cache: approximately 15 minutes.
  • Survey responses: normally up to 24 months, unless a shorter period is sufficient or a longer period is needed for documented research or legal purposes.
  • Analytics: controlled by ARTEZIQ’s GA4 retention settings. ARTEZIQ intends to use a limited retention period and does not use GA4 to store Explorer form content.

The applicant’s emailed copy of a signed NDA is under the applicant’s control. Backup systems may retain deleted information for a limited period before normal backup rotation completes.

Send requests to arteziq.apps@gmail.com with enough information for ARTEZIQ to identify the relevant record. ARTEZIQ may need to verify your identity before completing a request. People in the EEA/UK also have the right to complain to the data-protection authority responsible for their location. ARTEZIQ asks that you contact us first when practical so we have an opportunity to address the concern.

10. Analytics choice, regional detection, and local storage

ARTEZIQ uses a coarse country lookup to decide whether the stricter analytics-consent flow should be shown. The site requests a country determination from Country.is (api.country.is) on each page visit. Like any Internet request, the visitor’s IP address is visible to that service while the request is processed. ARTEZIQ does not store the returned IP address and does not cache the regional classification in the visitor’s browser. The country is checked again on each page load so a VPN change or international travel does not reuse a previous country result. Country.is states that its public service does not log requests.

For visitors detected in the EEA, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Google Analytics does not load until the visitor chooses Allow analytics. For visitors outside those regions, GA4 may load automatically unless the visitor has previously declined analytics. If the country cannot be determined, ARTEZIQ does not load GA4 and does not display the automatic consent interface. This avoids incorrectly treating an EEA/UK/Swiss visitor as outside the consent-required regions.

Visitors in the EEA, United Kingdom, or Switzerland are shown a centered Privacy & Analytics text link immediately above the site copyright so they can change their analytics choice. Advertising storage, ad-user-data, and ad-personalization signals remain denied by ARTEZIQ’s site configuration.

To change your analytics choice at any time, open analytics preferences.
