How to Play

Play Your Way

Drag pieces from the Tackle Tray and leave them anywhere on the table. Make piles, sort by color or shape, or build small groups before moving them to the puzzle frame.

Start With the Edges

Edges to Table scatters every loose edge piece onto the tabletop. Sort them into top, bottom, left, right, and corner groups if that is how you like to start.

Connect Pieces Anywhere

Correct neighboring pieces snap together even when they are away from the frame. Once connected, the group moves as one unit.

Use the Whole Table

All to Table scatters every loose tray piece. The same button then becomes Recall Singles, which sends only unconnected single pieces back to the tray.

Sort the Tray

Use the tray filter to show edges, corners, or pieces with 0–4 outward tabs. One-tab pieces can also be filtered by direction.

Snap to the Frame

When a piece or connected group reaches its correct place in the puzzle frame, it snap-locks into position. Finish the puzzle to reveal the species information and add it to your Fish Cooler.
