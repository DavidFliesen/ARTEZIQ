Angler's Arrows

Fish Identification Puzzle Game

Angler's Arrows turns fish identification into a fast, visual puzzle. Clear the arrows to reveal the catch, build your Fish Caught collection, and learn the visual clues that help you recognize different species on the water. It is designed to make learning fish identification approachable and fun while giving anglers another way to sharpen their knowledge between trips.

  • Quick, approachable arrow puzzles
  • Fish revealed as each level is solved
  • Species identification tips
  • Growing Fish Caught collection
  • Hints, undo, and reset controls
  • Being developed for iOS and Android
