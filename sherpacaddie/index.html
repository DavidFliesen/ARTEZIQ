Sherpa Caddie

Your Guide. Your Game. Your Best Round.

Sherpa Caddie is a beginner-friendly golf companion that combines the practical help of a caddie with the guidance of a coach. It helps you choose a club, read the course, pick safer targets, check conditions, and stay focused one shot at a time. Built for newer and recreational golfers, Sherpa keeps things simple: slow down, make smarter decisions, learn as you play, and treat every shot as a fresh start.

  • Club selection and shot guidance
  • Course strategy and safer targets
  • Weather and playing-condition awareness
  • Simple coaching and mental-game focus
  • Being developed for iOS and Android
